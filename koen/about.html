Our Story

The KOEN Atelier

Born from the ancient traditions of Indian perfumery, reimagined for the modern connoisseur.

Pure Essence, Zero Alcohol

Most modern perfumes are 80% alcohol, which evaporates quickly and can irritate the skin. At KOEN, we return to the roots of the 'Attar' tradition. Our perfumes are 100% oil-based, ensuring a scent that stays closer to your skin and lasts significantly longer.

Each bottle is a concentrated masterpiece, requiring only a few drops to create an aura that lasts from dawn until dusk.

Indian Heritage

India has been the cradle of perfumery for millennia. From the sandalwood of the South to the roses of Kannauj, our land is rich with olfactory treasures. KOEN celebrates this heritage by sourcing the finest natural ingredients across the subcontinent.
